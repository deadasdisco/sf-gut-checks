Key Drivers of Success & Drop-Off

Positive Drivers (What Won 4s & 5s)

  • Authentic Photography: Real, relatable human faces consistently generated connection and high ratings.
  • Micro-Interactions: Subtle hover states that expand info and subtle image movement were viewed as modern and premium.
  • Clear Front-Page Structure: Prominent event calendars, direct program links, and mission statements above the fold anchored user orientation.

Friction Points (What Triggered 1s & 2s)

  • Cartoons & Emojis: Diminished credibility and made the sites appear informal or juvenile.
  • Premature Donation Demands: Asking for money before establishing clear mission value repelled first-time visitors.
  • Small Font & Low Contrast: Tight fonts and low-contrast palettes significantly impaired readability.
